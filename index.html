Event Overview

Get ready for an exciting and knowledge-packed day at the AWS Community Day Pune AI / ML Edition 2025! This exclusive event brings together the brightest minds in AI and ML for a day full of inspiration, learning, and networking. Whether you're a developer, data scientist, or an AI enthusiast, this conference is your opportunity to explore the latest in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning powered by Amazon Web Services (AWS).
